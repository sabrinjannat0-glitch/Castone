Apply for your ARC - Guide & Requirements

MANDATORY IMMIGRATION REQUIREMENTS (2026)

Must be completed within 90 days of arrival in South Korea

Required Documents

  1. Application Form
  2. Passport Copy & Original Passport
  3. ID Photo (1 photo)
    • Size: 3.5 x 4.5 cm
    • Taken within the last 6 months
    • White background
  4. Application Fee: 50,000 KRW
  5. Housing Contract / Proof of Residence
    Dormitory Addresses:

    • On-Campus Dorm: 309 Suyeong-ro, Nam-gu, Busan (Kyungsung Univ. Nuri Dorm 1)

    • Off-Campus Dorm: 63-3 Yongso-ro 7beon-gil, Nam-gu, Busan

Special Housing Scenarios

👥 If you live with a friend:
  • House contract
  • Copy of friend's ARC / passport
  • Completed Confirmation of Residence form
🏢 If you live in a Gosiwon / Airbnb:
  • House contract
  • Photocopy of owner's ID card
  • Completed Confirmation of Residence form
  • Owner's Business Registration Certificate
  • Most recent proof of rent payment
